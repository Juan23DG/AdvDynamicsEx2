Quick-Start Guide

How to Use This Site to Study Effectively

💡 Your step progress & theme are saved automatically in your browser
1

Choose Guided, Self-Check Only, or Read-Through Mode

Use 🎯 Guided Tutor for step-by-step hints & intuition, switch to ⚡ Self-Check Only to solve broken-down steps with no hints or help (pure self-checks), or use 📖 Full Read-Through to expand all derivations at once.

2

Follow the Sticky Problem & Color Timeline

As you scroll down through a multi-step derivation, the Problem Statement, SVG Diagram, and Parameters follow your scroll on the left (or via 📌 Peek Problem on tablet/mobile). Each step is color-coded so you always see where a new step begins.

3

Unlock Progressive Hints & Textbook Links

Every step includes a Step Goal, Physical Intuition, 💡 Hint 1 (Setup & Strategy), and 🧮 Hint 2 (Key Math & Chain-Rule Check), plus clickable Concept Chips and verified links to LibreTexts, MIT OCW, CMU, and FSU lecture videos.

4

Test Yourself on Randomized Checkpoints

Each step has a 4-option (A, B, C, D) checkpoint with randomized answer positions. Selecting a distractor reveals a targeted Diagnostic Hint explaining the exact sign error or missing term (e.g., forgetting $\dot{\mathbf{J}}\dot{\mathbf{q}}$ or $2\dot{\mathbf{r}}_{\text{cart}}\cdot\dot{\mathbf{r}}_{\text{rel}}$).
